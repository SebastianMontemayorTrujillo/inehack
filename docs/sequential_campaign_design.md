# Sequential interaction and citizen-campaign design

Approved 27 October 2026. Audience: beginning ethics/civic-education learners in Mexico. This amendment supersedes floor labels, shared host/object actions and the earlier generic campaign-ending panel. Acceptance is recorded only after actual checks in `verification.md` and RV21–RV26.

## Route and affordances

All houses are enterable, but learning follows Luz (left) → Mateo (center) → Inés (right) → Luz's press (left) → FIN. Early hosts name the first unfinished activity and give a direction. Completed hosts give the next destination and offer optional review. Physical WASD entrances/exits remain the only Godot travel. Neither E/Enter nor a panel action changes location.

Characters begin lessons. The noticeboard shows its pending claim or sourced correction/publication; the ballot box previews projects before prerequisites and opens the unlocked consultation afterwards. The printing machine is exclusively for the final campaign. It never speaks as Luz or begins her activity. All five consultation actions unlock the same finish.

One resolved nearest target supplies the nearby `[E]` box and contextual-button label and is checked again when activating. No target or an open panel hides the box. The floor carries no prose: location and one objective live in the HUD; data, sources and conclusions remain inspectable through panels/diary. Doors retain visual markers and movement areas.

The six stable activity IDs and exact-data rules remain. Prompts ask one plain question; introductory speech explains a record and a percentage. Unknown classification remains unknown, counts and proportions stay distinct, and invented examples are explicitly separate from INE data. Assistance/retry remains available without claiming independent mastery.

The selected direction recalls earlier INE civic materials: project pink `#B41476`, white `#FFFFFF`, ink `#242124`, pale pink `#FBEAF3`. These are project choices, not certified official tokens or the current identity manual. The [2021 INE editorial reference](https://ine.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/DECEyEC_Manual-de-estilo-INE-actualizado.pdf) is historical visual context. The user explicitly selected the familiar pink direction after being informed of the September 2026 identity changes.

The original citizen-campaign poster uses a large invitation, a sourced finding, common-scale labelled bars, a discussion prompt and a readable source/limits footer. Retain exact counts, bases and NS, year, source link and searchable vector Carta output. No official logo, Latas/INEhack branding or synthetic consultation tally appears. Use the existing libre Atkinson fonts. Shared layout/color resources bind PDF and preview designs; native/JavaScript PDF outputs must agree.

Printing records completion once, then reveals a sheet/finished poster in no more than 2.5 seconds. E, Enter or a visible skip control reaches FIN immediately. Reduced motion skips animation. The final heading and Download/Enlarge/Explore controls stay available without scrolling; the enlarged poster has its own scroll region. Reopening skips the reveal; closing restores the same physical position. No download or physical printer is required for in-game completion. Frozen jobs, repeated downloads, replacement/cancellation and private persistence retain the campaign contract.
